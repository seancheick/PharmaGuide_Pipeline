## 1. Root cause — exact, and reproduced from the record

`201420` (Solgar Male Multiple) declares one folate exposure and its own form
breakdown, and the two rows are **both transcribed `Folic Acid`**:

| Row | Raw text | Amount | Basis | %DV | Enricher exposure anchor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| declared total (top-level) | `Folic Acid` | 1333 | mcg DFE | 333% | `daily_value_confirmed_nutrient_amount` |
| nested breakdown | `Folic Acid` | 800 | mcg | — | `canonical_parent_substance_amount` |

Sibling record `201405` is the **same printed panel** and transcribes the total
as `Folate 1333 mcg DFE`. So the source was never ambiguous — that was settled in
the source-resolution phase.

The defect was in the dose-safety contract's *identification* step. It decided
which contributing row owns the declared total from a **parent-name
vocabulary** (`_FOLATE_PARENT_NAMES = {folate, vitamin b9 folate, vitamin b9}`).
`Folic Acid` is not in it, so no row was recognised as the total and both rows
were treated as separate discharges. The decisive proof is the sibling:

| Record | Folate contributing rows | Contract verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `201405` | `Folate` 1333 DFE + `Folic Acid` 1360 DFE | `not_applicable` — correct |
| `201420` | `Folic Acid` 1333 DFE + `Folic Acid` 1360 DFE | `confirmed_over_threshold`, **penalized** — wrong |

Identical numbers, identical panel, opposite treatment — decided solely by a
label's spelling of the total's name.

## 2. Structural fix — no product-specific bypass, no row rewrite

`is_folate_parent_total_duplicate_flag` now identifies the declared total from
**structure**, in tiers, and only when the evidence singles one row out:

1. the label names the row as the nutrient total (unchanged, existing
   vocabulary);
2. **exactly one** row is anchored to a Daily Value
   (`ul_exposure_basis = daily_value_confirmed_nutrient_amount`) — a row carrying
   a %DV *is* the label's nutrient declaration, whatever DSLD called it;
3. the rows stated in the declared-total (DFE) basis all carry **one name**, so
   they are one printed row emitted several times (the serving-variant shape
   `243808` already relies on).

A shape the contract cannot single out — two Daily-Value-anchored rows, or
several differently named DFE rows — is left unreconciled, because summing is
the conservative answer there. Nothing is keyed on a DSLD id, and no row value,
unit or name was modified.

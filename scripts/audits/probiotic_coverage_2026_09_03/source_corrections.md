# Source corrections — verified 2026-09-04

Read the complete NLM PubMed XML abstracts through EFetch individually before
editing these records. These corrections do not constitute clinician sign-off.

- HN019: [39356506](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39356506/) tested 7.0 billion
  CFU/day at start, 4.69 billion at end, for eight weeks in adults with functional
  constipation. Primary complete spontaneous bowel movement change was not
  superior to placebo. Secondary [40320938](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40320938/)
  also found no superiority on that endpoint. Classify those sources as null,
  not the strain as universally ineffective; remove unsupported 10–20B copy and
  the secondary transit-time-reduction headline. Existing support grade describes
  study strength, not positive effect. No dose window added.
- Bi-07: [17408927](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17408927/) concerns laboratory
  rifaximin resistance in **B. infantis BI07**, not the registered **B. lactis
  Bi-07**. Remove the wrong citation and suspend that native review approval.
  Do not replace it with the NCFM/Bi-07 combination paper as independent evidence.
- BB-12: PMID 38271203 was not returned by NLM or Europe PMC during the bounded
  check. Remove the unverified citation and suspend native approval. Per the
  registry's clinician-review rule, do not swap in another PMID automatically.
  Verified candidate [26382580](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26382580/): original
  responder endpoint P=.071 and global discomfort endpoint not significant;
  revised responder criterion and average frequency favorable. This is **mixed**
  evidence, not an unconditional positive trial. Keep it in the research queue.
  Tested 1B and 10B doses do not establish a continuous range, and do not reveal
  individual BB-12 exposure in Ritual's combined blend.

Both suspended entries retain their identity/aliases. Historic references and
approvals remain recoverable in git; neither is presented as current validation.
Missing review is a knowledge gap, not a negative finding about the product.

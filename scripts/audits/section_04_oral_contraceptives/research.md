# Section 4 — oral contraceptives: scope and citation review

## Runtime scope decision

`class:oral_contraceptives` is currently a systemic hormonal-contraceptive
class. The Flutter bridge resolves selected, generic, and ingredient RxCUIs, but
does not preserve route or formulation. A class based on ethinyl estradiol plus
progestin ingredients would therefore also match the patch and vaginal ring;
product-only RxCUIs would miss routine ingredient hydration. No narrow
combined-oral class is authored until that identity model is route-aware.

All five Section 4 records consequently remain non-emitted. This is not a claim
that the relationships are false; it is a failure-closed scope decision.

## Literature disposition

| Record | Content review | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| B12 | PMID 21967158 addresses oral contraceptives and serum status, but a serum/binding-protein change does not establish functional deficiency or a supplement indication. | Rejected |
| Magnesium | PMID 23636014 is unrelated to both oral contraception and magnesium. | Needs revision, suppressed |
| Zinc | PMID 23636014 is unrelated to both oral contraception and zinc. | Needs revision, suppressed |
| Vitamin C | The inherited textbook claim lacks exact modern, clinically meaningful deficiency evidence. PMID 7001015 is an older review only. | Rejected |
| Vitamin E | PMID 7001015 reports little or no alpha-tocopherol effect; it contradicts a depletion warning. | Rejected |

No enrichment, scoring, dashboard rebuild, or interaction-DB release is needed:
no runtime class is added or repointed, and every Section 4 entry is suppressed.

## Bounded source assessment

- [Chandrasekhar 2012, PMID23439798](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3573577/): actual KSM-66 root-only extract from Ixoreal, at least 5% withanolides by HPLC; 64 randomized adults with elevated stress, 61 efficacy analyses; 600 mg/day for 60 days. Perceived-stress scales favored extract versus placebo. No single prespecified primary scale is invented. Small, short, single-center, per-protocol evidence does not establish long-term benefit, psychiatric-disease treatment or other extracts' bioequivalence.
- [Salve 2019, PMID32021735](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6979308/): actual gifted KSM-66 batch, 60 randomized stressed adults, 58 completed; 250 or 600 mg/day for eight weeks. Perceived stress improved versus placebo. Anxiety findings differed between assigned arms. Sleep was self-reported on a seven-point scale, not objective sleep measurement or proof of insomnia treatment. Independent constituent assay is not asserted from this paper.

Official full source bodies and independent source review were reused; no literature search was restarted. These are **two primary trial families**, not four independent replications after adding overlapping syntheses. The unread preparation table of PMID39348746 remains unverified. Both retained trials studied KSM-66; this batch does not claim direct testing of a diverse standardized-extract class.

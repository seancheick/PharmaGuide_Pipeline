# Section 9 — specialized therapies review

Reviewed: 2026-07-26

## Verified, direct-label effects

- Orlistat → vitamins A, D, E, K: current DailyMed XENICAL label directs every patient to take a multivitamin with fat-soluble vitamins, explains reduced absorption, and requires separation by at least two hours.
- Cholestyramine → vitamins A, D, E, K: current DailyMed label says the resin can prevent absorption of fat-soluble vitamins and recommends considering water-miscible or parenteral supplementation with long-term use.

## Suppressed pending narrow, claim-matched evidence

- Calcineurin inhibitors → magnesium: plausible direct renal effect, but the currently cited PMID is already content-rejected; retain suppression until a verified replacement and a narrower calcineurin scope are authored.
- Broad immunosuppressants → vitamin D: transplant-associated bone/mineral status is not evidence that every listed immunosuppressant causes a vitamin-D depletion alert.
- HIV protease inhibitors → vitamin D and zinc: current citations were already content-mismatched and the records conflate HIV disease, treatment, and individual-agent effects.

No new taxonomy is promoted. Direct-drug RxCUIs were already established; all class claims remain suppressed.

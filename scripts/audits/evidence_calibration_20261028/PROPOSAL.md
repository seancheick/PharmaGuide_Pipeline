## 4. Decisions for Sean

1. **Approve the rule (config 1.31.0)?** Brand markers and the allowlist no longer lift Evidence; one trial is a moderate floor; 19–20 needs a replicated reviewed body. Consequence: 0 records qualify today, so 788 products lose 4.4 points (creatine and vitamin D included) until the clinical lane records three clinician-approved, independent, consistent trial families for one condition on a record. Creatine's evidence body is plausibly exceptional. Our registry holds one approved pooled meta-analysis context for it, and by the existing probiotic owner rule a pooled record earns no replication credit until its trials are linked. Alternative for Dr Pham: let a clinician-approved meta-analysis of ≥3 RCTs with consistent direction count as replicated. That is a policy change to the shared consistency owner and would also affect probiotics.
2. **Enrollment in the floors (measured, not implemented).** The strong/moderate floors ignore enrollment. Applying the existing bands (×0.8 for n < 200, ×0.6 for n < 50) would move about 420 more products, for example INGR_TURMERIC (n = 90, 119 products at 15.6) and BRAND_KSM66 (n = 124), and needs a fail-closed rule for the 384 floor products whose record has no enrollment. That is a policy owner decision.
3. **Nutrition panels at 19–20: a separate pathway, not a defect of this batch.** Multi/prenatal (1,332) and B-complex (125) complete panels are scored by their own panel owners (`multi_prenatal_evidence`, the B-complex panel route). That is coverage of an essential-nutrient panel, a different assessment from one nutrient's authority floor, so these weights stay unchanged here. Their pipeline copy is accurate: "Established nutritional authority recognizes the physiological necessity of these essential nutrients." Presentation item for separate review: the app's generic Evidence status chip (`lib/core/scoring/v4_pillars.dart::statusForPillar`, ≥85% = "Strong") shows the same "Strong" label for nutritional necessity as for replicated clinical efficacy.
4. **Purpose-scoped replication: implemented as a hold (`26d1c9928`).** A replicated context counts only if its condition falls in an indication category the label declares for that ingredient (role owner) or, with no declaration, one the record's reviewed outcomes support. The categorizer is the shared `evidence_indication_categories`. A condition it cannot place stays held. Limitation for Dr Pham: that categorizer has no joint, musculoskeletal or sports category ("knee osteoarthritis" and "resistance training" map to nothing), and it puts sleep and stress together under "mood". So creatine, joint and sports bodies cannot reach 19–20 until the vocabulary covers them, and any extension is a clinical-owner decision.
5. **Record follow-ups for the clinical lane, not changed here:** INGR_VITAMIN_D3 is labelled positive_strong systematic_review_meta, but its only context (respiratory infection) is mixed/null and pending review; it still floors 454 products at 15.6. BRAND_ASTAREAL is `rct_multiple` with one PMID. Bands at the edges: positive_weak strong-design floors land at 10.4 (between limited and moderate).

6. **Omega-3 `purpose_evidence` re-basis (requested by the clinical-closure lane; proposal only, no file edited).** Ordinary adult EPA+DHA products earn 10.4 via `INGR_OMEGA3.purpose_evidence.omega_reviewed_weak`, which cites heart-outcome reviews (PMIDs 31567003, 32951855). Dr Pham removed routine retail heart-event credit but kept EPA/DHA nutritional credit. Proposed, for the clinical lane's file:
   - Replace `omega_reviewed_weak` with an adult EPA+DHA nutritional-intake standard. Proposed source, verified at the publisher page: EFSA NDA Panel (2010), *Dietary Reference Values for fats*, EFSA Journal 8(3):1461, doi:10.2903/j.efsa.2010.1461. It sets an Adequate Intake of 250 mg EPA+DHA/day for adults and 100–200 mg extra preformed DHA in pregnancy and lactation. It is a dietary reference value, not an RDA and not a clinical-outcome claim; it has no PMID, so the record would cite the DOI.
   - Value options, measured on 714 omega-route products (stored enriched inputs, candidate code): **A (recommended)** keep 10.4 and re-cite only, with 0 score movers; 420 products change only the cited record. **B** align with the prenatal DHA intake authority (11.1), giving 420 products +0.7.
   - Disclosure rule (Dr Pham: fish, krill and algal EPA/DHA all count when disclosed). Today 84 adult products that disclose a trustworthy EPA/DHA amount are held at 0, because the gate in `evidence_resolver.resolve_omega_evidence_standard` requires both EPA and DHA rows: 50 combined-EPA+DHA or EPA-only products and 34 DHA-only products (e.g. Algal-900 DHA, Vegan DHA from Algae). Counting any disclosed EPA and/or DHA, measured in a throwaway shadow checkout: 84 products go 0 → 10.4, no other pillar moves, 53 tier changes (all up). The 56 products that disclose no EPA/DHA amount (e.g. "Fish Oil 1000 mg") stay held. One to review: 82325 "Phosphatidyl Serine Plus DHA" qualifies as omega-only ownership. The `evidence_resolver.py` edit belongs to the next owner change; the field-cleanup lane is also editing that file.
   - Triple Omega 179650 and 302769 (generic route): the raw DSLD rows disclose only "Omega-3 Fatty Acids / Total Omega-3 434 mg" from fish, flaxseed, safflower and olive oils.

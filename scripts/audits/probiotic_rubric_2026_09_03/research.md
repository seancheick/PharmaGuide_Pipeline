# Seed DS-01 publication review — 2026-09-03

Primary full texts were read through Europe PMC's fullTextXML endpoint:
[PMID 41599868](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12845427/),
[PMID 40944126](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12430871/), and
[PMID 41750436](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12937403/).
Registration cross-check: [NCT04171466](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04171466).

- 41599868: six-week adult self-reported GI-symptom trial; 350 baseline,
  219 at week six. The scoring enrollment remains 350, not a sum of papers.
- 40944126: 91-day healthy-adult mechanistic study, 32 randomized, 27 completed.
- 41750436: 91-day antibiotic-challenge mechanistic study, 32 randomized,
  21 completed (11 lost). First seven days included ciprofloxacin and
  metronidazole. Endpoints were microbial diversity, metabolites and barrier
  function. This is NOT evidence of preventing antibiotic-associated diarrhea.
- The latter two publications explicitly share a master protocol. The live
  registration has four antibiotic/no-antibiotic arms and actual enrollment 46.
  Publication counts do not reconcile into an independently established unique
  participant count. Do not assert participant independence or sum to 64.
- Sponsor involvement and attrition remain limitations. Neither mechanistic
  paper is independent replication of clinical symptom benefit.

All three report DS-01, 24 strains, 53.6 billion AFU, 400 mg pomegranate,
and capsule-in-capsule delivery. The symptom trial uses one capsule for the
first three days, then two daily; the matching contract addresses the maintenance
dose. AFU is never converted to CFU.

Important material limitation: papers report the full strain list and total
potency, not the commercial label's four per-blend AFU allocations. Those
allocations remain a conservative commercial-label identity constraint, not
independently published trial quantities. A matching product is described as a
reviewed commercial formula matching reported composition and total dose, not
proof of exact per-strain trial proportions.

Disposition: add 41750436 as a related mechanistic publication, retain
`rct_single`, `positive_weak`, medium evidence certainty and enrollment 350.
Do not award publication-count/depth credit or new clinical outcomes.

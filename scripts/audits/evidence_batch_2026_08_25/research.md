# Evidence batch 2026-08-25 — inositol and L-arginine

This batch was selected from the post-materiality readiness backlog because
`inositol` and `l_arginine` are among the highest-exposure unresolved canonical
identities. Counts are prioritization signals, not evidence of efficacy.

## Inositol

- PMID 38163998: 30 PCOS trials, 2,230 participants. The guideline-informing
  systematic review found possible benefits for selected outcomes but concluded
  that evidence was limited and inconclusive.
- PMID 36703143: 26 RCTs, 1,691 participants. The meta-analysis reported benefits
  for cycle normalization and selected metabolic/hormonal outcomes.
- PMID 35477841: 17 RCTs, 1,083 participants. The meta-analysis found no clinically
  significant improvement across most anthropometric, metabolic, and endocrine
  outcomes and reported high heterogeneity.

Decision: `mixed`, high confidence in the classification. Scope is myo-inositol,
D-chiro-inositol, and explicitly named combinations only. Inositol hexanicotinate,
inositol hexaphosphate/IP6, and unrelated inositol compounds are excluded.

## L-arginine

- PMID 34967840: 22 placebo-controlled RCTs. The dose-response meta-analysis
  reported lower blood pressure overall, with an effective SBP dose detected at
  4 g/day and important subgroup/dose limitations.
- PMID 22137067: 11 randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trials, 387
  participants, using 4–24 g/day; pooled blood-pressure reductions were reported.
- PMID 32370176: 18 athletic-performance studies (15 meta-analyzed). Results were
  positive on average but heterogeneous; the paper described endpoint-specific
  acute and chronic dose protocols from 1.5–12 g/day.

Decision: `mixed`, high confidence in the classification because benefits are
endpoint- and dose-specific and the sports literature is heterogeneous. This
batch is restricted to free/base/micronized L-arginine. AAKG, nitrate, silicate,
HCl, aspartate, and other compounds/forms remain unreviewed rather than borrowing
generic evidence.

All six PMIDs, exact titles, abstracts, publication types, DOI values, and
retraction flags were fetched from PubMed E-utilities on 2026-08-25.

FDA GSRS was also queried live on 2026-08-25. It resolved Inositol to UNII
`4L6452S749` (CAS 87-89-8, RxCUI 5833) and Arginine to UNII `94ZLA3W45F`
(CAS 74-79-3, RxCUI 1091, PubChem CID 6322). The arginine response also listed
distinct salt/parent relationships, supporting this batch's decision not to
transfer the free/base record to named arginine compounds automatically.

## Reason fixes found on the way

- **BANNED_S23**: S-23 is a synthetic non-steroidal selective androgen receptor modulator (SARM) developed as an investigational male contraceptive candidate; in male rats it suppressed LH and FSH and, combined with estradiol, reversibly stopped sperm production; no human data were found. It is not approved by any regulatory agency, not a dietary ingredient,  No NDI notification is on file. WADA prohibits it. FDA treats S-23 products as unapproved new drugs, and their contraceptive and androgenic effects are not declared on labels.  
  → S-23 is a synthetic non-steroidal selective androgen receptor modulator (SARM) developed as an investigational male contraceptive candidate; in male rats it suppressed LH and FSH and, combined with estradiol, reversibly stopped sperm production; no human data were found. It is not approved by any regulatory agency, not a dietary ingredient. No NDI notification is on file. WADA prohibits it. FDA treats S-23 products as unapproved new drugs, and their contraceptive and androgenic effects are not declared on labels.
- **NOOTROPIC_BROMANTANE**: Bromantane (Ladasten) is a synthetic actoprotective compound with stimulant and anxiolytic properties developed in the Soviet Union. FDA has not approved it and has not accepted it as a dietary ingredient. No NDI notification is on file. WADA prohibits it as a stimulant. In rats it increases dopamine synthesis and release. Human data come mainly from Russian clinical studies of 50-100 mg a day for about four weeks, so long-term and high-dose safety is not established. Products marketing it as a US supplement are adulterated.

- **RECALLED_RHEUMACARE_CAPSULES**: Rheumacare Capsules were found to contain extreme lead contamination — up to 11,100 ppm (milligrams per kilogram) of lead — thousands of times above any acceptable level for a dietary supplement. Lead at this concentration causes acute and chronic lead poisoning: neurological damage, kidney injury, bone disruption, and in children severe developmental harm. California Prop 65's maximum level is 0.5 mcg/day total lead; a single capsule of this product would deliver a catastrophically toxic dose. FDA issued an urgent recall.  
  → Rheumacare Capsules were found to contain lead at up to 11,100 ppm (milligrams per kilogram) in FDA testing. - **RECALLED_JACK3D**: Jack3d (USPlabs) contained DMAA (1,3-dimethylamylamine) and was recalled. Two US soldiers who collapsed and died during exercise had been taking DMAA-containing supplements, and case reports describe a hemorrhagic stroke and a heart attack in young men taking Jack3d. The US Army banned Jack3d from military installations in 2012. The Department of Defense subsequently launched a comprehensive supplement safety review. FDA issued warning letters and worked with USPlabs to remove DMAA-containing products. This case drove DMAA's formal classification as an unlawful dietary ingredient.

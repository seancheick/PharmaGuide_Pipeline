# PharmaGuide Scoring Engine Specification

Read the current versions from `SCORING_ENGINE_VERSION` in `score_supplements_v4.py`,
`_metadata.version` in `scoring_v4/config/quality_score.json`,
`SCORED_ARTIFACT_SCHEMA_VERSION` in `scoring_v4/scored_artifact.py`, and
`EXPORT_SCHEMA_VERSION` in `build_final_db.py`. These source stamps own versions.

## 1. Scope

This specification defines the scoring behavior that is allowed to ship. Code
remains authoritative when this document and implementation disagree.

Primary implementation:

- `score_supplements_v4.py`
- `scoring_input_contract.py` (ScoringClassification v1 route decision)
- `scoring_v4/router.py` (adapter; classification owner moved to the contract)
- `scoring_v4/gate_safety.py`
- `scoring_v4/gate_completeness.py`
- `scoring_v4/modules/`
- `scoring_v4/quality_score.py`
- `scoring_v4/scored_artifact.py`
- `score_products_v4.py`
- `build_final_db.py`

The production score is v4 `/100`. Stage 3 produces the complete v4 artifact;
final export consumes it directly and never invokes a second scorer.

For a team-facing explanation of all six pillars, category differences, exact omega tables, worked arithmetic and calibration review questions, see the [October 17 scoring review packet](../docs/scoring/SCORING_TEAM_REVIEW_2026-10-17.md). It describes source behavior and distinguishes it from the currently delivered catalog.

## 2. Non-negotiable invariants

1. `quality_score_v4_100` is the only canonical public score.
2. `quality_score_status` governs whether a numeric score may ship.
3. BLOCKED/UNSAFE products never ship a rankable score.
4. NOT_SCORED products never receive a public quality number or tier. Export
   may retain a verified source label with a documented unfinished
   assessment and visible warnings; unusable identity/payload is quarantined.
5. `raw_score_v4_100` is audit-only and never fills a suppressed public field.
6. `score_100_equivalent` and its display field mirror v4; they are not a
   conversion of the legacy score.
7. Deprecated `/80` columns remain absent.
8. One v4 router owns module dispatch.
9. One shared scoring-input contract owns which ingredient rows reach scoring.
10. Matching and clinical identity decisions occur upstream; scorers do not
    introduce fuzzy identity fallbacks.
11. Every output includes reproducible engine/config/classification provenance.
12. Per-product regression deltas must be attributable before snapshots move.

## 3. Inputs

V4 consumes the enriched product contract generated by
`enrich_supplements_v3.py`. It does not parse raw DSLD labels independently.

### 3.1 Shared ingredient input

`scoring_input_contract.py` is the only scoring-row boundary. It consumes
cleaner-owned role/eligibility fields and emits:

- score-eligible ingredient rows
- stable identity and source linkage
- blend/parent-total/display-only exclusions
- mapped coverage
- diagnostics and strict contract metadata

The same contract is used by pre-score validation, v4, the artifact assembler,
and export diagnostics. A downstream consumer may explain an exclusion but may not
reclassify the row with a second heuristic.

### 3.2 Required upstream domains

Depending on category, the scorer reads structured blocks including:

- ingredient quality and canonical identity
- product taxonomy/classification
- dose and serving exposure
- safety signals and regulatory applicability
- RDA/AI/UL assessments
- clinical evidence
- proprietary blend/disclosure evidence
- certifications, COA, testing, GMP, and traceability
- manufacturer violations
- probiotics, omega-3, sports, and formulation-specific metadata

Missing optional evidence lowers confidence or pillar credit. Missing usable
identity/payload can trigger NOT_SCORED. The scorer must never invent evidence
to avoid a null.

## 4. Execution model

`score_product_v4()` executes these layers in order:

1. route product to one module
2. evaluate canonical safety signals
3. short-circuit hard safety conditions
4. evaluate class-aware completeness
5. short-circuit unusable products
6. run exactly one category module
7. apply completeness policy and verdict precedence
8. evaluate confidence
9. attach engine/config provenance
10. assemble six public pillars, score, tier, and status
11. project the complete Stage-3 artifact once

The input dict is not mutated.

## 5. Routing

`scoring_input_contract.py::build_scoring_classification()` is the sole
route authority (ScoringClassification v1, `SCORING_CLASSIFICATION_SCHEMA_VERSION`);
`scoring_v4/router.py::class_for_product()` is a thin adapter that calls it and
raises on a classifier failure (no silent `generic` fallback).
Routes are pinned by `SCORING_ROUTE_MODULES` in the same file.

The decision order below is a summary, not a spec — the classifier is the truth.
Verified route decision order (2026-09-18):

| Order | Route | Trigger (summary) |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | `probiotic` | Probiotic-class identity/CFU evidence (unless greens-powder primary type); a title that declares a multi ("Multi + Probiotic") over a multi-eligible panel routes `multi_or_prenatal` instead (closure D4); as on every non-probiotic route, the strains add no Evidence points and their disposition/completeness still comes from the probiotic owner (`assess_probiotic_component_disposition`) |
| 2 | `omega` | Prenatal title whose panel is genuinely omega-primary |
| 3 | `multi_or_prenatal` | Prenatal title with multi-panel/taxonomy intent |
| 4 | `sports` | Sports identity or primary-dose evidence |
| 5 | `generic` | Protein title intent with no protein mass/collagen identity (evidence-missing containment) |
| 6 | `omega` | Omega product types passing `_route_is_omega_class` |
| 7 | `b_complex` | B-complex panel decision |
| 8 | `multi_or_prenatal` | Explicit multivitamin name / multivitamin taxonomy (eligibility-guarded) |
| 9 | `fiber_digestive` | Fiber/digestive decision |
| 10 | taxonomy-specific | Taxonomy claims validated by class evidence (omega/sports); else demoted to `generic` with an evidence-missing reason |
| 11 | `omega` / `generic` | Late omega-class check, then generic safe default |

Routing prefers canonical taxonomy and panel composition; a taxonomy claim
without class evidence demotes to `generic` with an explicit reason rather than
scoring in the claimed module. Product-name signals are bounded and guarded.
The router does not import the legacy scorer.

## 6. Safety gate

`identity/safety.py` normalizes raw evidence into the stable safety-signal
contract. `gate_safety.py` applies policy to `match_resolution`, status,
jurisdiction, and role; it does not branch on matcher implementation names.

| Evidence | V4 action |
|---|---|
| Confirmed banned | BLOCKED; scoring short-circuits |
| Confirmed recalled | UNSAFE; scoring short-circuits |
| Confirmed/likely high-risk or watchlist | CAUTION; score math continues |
| Likely banned/recalled | CAUTION + review; no automatic hard block |
| Review-only/low-confidence | Audit/review metadata; no unsupported verdict |
| Inactive `excipient_acceptable` warning | Visible warning; no false hard block |
| Disease claim | CAUTION |
| Material indeterminate folate UL | CAUTION + review without asserting exceedance |

Primary verdict applicability is US federal/state. Regional evidence remains in
`jurisdictions` and `regional_advisories`.

The final export also enforces banned-substance parity without deciding the
verdict itself: `build_final_db.py::project_export_scored_artifact` raises if
the export's own critical banned warning is not matched by a Stage 3 `BLOCKED`
decision (verdict, safety verdict, product safety status and blocking reason),
or a recalled warning by `UNSAFE`. The verdict is decided once, in Stage 3; a
disagreement stops the build instead of shipping.

## 7. Completeness gate

`gate_completeness.py` is class-aware. It separates unusable data from honest
but incomplete disclosure.

Hard completeness failure:

- emits `v4_verdict=NOT_SCORED`
- emits `quality_score_status=not_scored`
- emits no public score or pillars
- is reviewed by the final export gate for verified-label publication or
  quarantine; the completeness result alone cannot authorize publication

Soft missing evidence can include dose, CFU, EPA/DHA detail, form factor, or
coverage debt. It is represented in the breakdown/confidence/pillar result and
does not justify fabricated values. The shared `mapped_coverage` source is used;
the v4 gate does not recalculate a competing definition.

## 8. Category modules

All routes are active and score-producing:

- generic
- probiotic
- multi/prenatal
- B-complex
- sports
- fiber/digestive
- omega-3

Modules return structured dimensions, components, penalties, metadata, and
raw score evidence. Category-specific math belongs inside the module/config,
not in the router or exporter.

## 9. Public pillar assembly

`scoring_v4/quality_score.py` builds:

| Pillar | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Formulation | 20 |
| Dose | 20 |
| Evidence | 20 |
| Transparency | 15 |
| Verification | 15 |
| Formula & quality checks (`safety_hygiene`) | 10 |

Pillars are category-aware, purpose-fit adapters, not linear remaps of module
dimensions: they normalize against reviewed structural ceilings per archetype
(`category_magnitudes` in the config), verification saturates hard third-party
signals with a fail-open neutral baseline, and only transparency remains a
faithful linear source-dimension map. Since config 1.9.0 the rubric proxies are
removed (ingredient-count breadth, focus bonuses/floors, generic enzyme
recognition, blanket gummy penalties, organic/Non-GMO/natural-source points,
the omega EPA:DHA ratio bonus, astaxanthin/CoQ10 caps, ingredient-presence
Evidence floors, certification-derived omega floors); fish/krill/algal carrier
mass never becomes EPA+DHA dose, and an omega row printed as EPA and/or DHA
owns that amount (1.10.x). Since engine 4.4.0, null earns no affirmative
Evidence credit (Wave 2) and Evidence norms use the primary-mass-floor
calibration (Wave 3). Dose and Evidence never manufacture Formulation credit.

Since config 1.26.0 (V4.1 Step 4) every fact earns or loses points once:

- Additive, dietary-sugar and confirmed high-risk/watchlist findings are
  charged only by the formula-checks pillar, within its shared 10-point budget.
  Low-severity findings are worded as formula quality.
- An upper-limit exceedance reduces only Dose, through one product-level ceiling (§11.3).
- Blend opacity is charged only in Transparency (B5). The Transparency base
  requires every active to have a resolved identity.
- Formulation credits form quality only:
  - A product or row with no form rating keeps one shared neutral (12/20).
  - A recognized botanical or collagen identity is neutral credit.
  - Presence, count, focus, route-base, disclosure and duplicate branded
    credits are retired, and references are the retained attainable maximum.
- Dose (Dose V4.1, config 1.29.1; plan `docs/plans/DOSE_V41_PLAN.md`):
  - Adequacy reads the minimum directed daily use and safety the maximum on
    every route; one Facts-panel serving a day is assessed, and recorded as the
    default, when the label gives no daily directions (§11.1).
  - Ordinary-adult references: magnesium 84 mg and calcium 260 mg elemental
    (PharmaGuide contribution targets, 20% of the FDA Daily Value), potassium a
    completed neutral, omega EPA+DHA 250 mg/day, an explicit triglyceride
    purpose 2,000 mg/day, prenatal products 200 mg/day DHA only. Applicable
    numerical contribution targets use `20 x min(amount / reference, 1)`;
    above a reference earns no extra credit. Potassium has no general
    supplement benchmark and receives the completed neutral instead.
  - Approved applicable contribution curves plateau above their reference;
    the highest studied dose is not an upper limit. A discrete studied regimen
    remains descriptive unless stronger amount semantics are established.
    Evidence certainty never caps Dose (ATP, betaine, taurine).
  - Generic declared purposes and sports co-primaries count equally; probiotic
    Dose averages the label's strains unless an exact studied formula applies.
  - An amount without an applicable benchmark, and an amount the label hides,
    earn the shared 11.4/20 neutral; Transparency keeps the disclosure
    consequence. A pipeline-lost amount keeps its strict readiness hold.
  - Active config and native-reference anchors carry source/curve semantics
    checked exhaustively by `scripts/tests/test_dose_reference_semantics.py`
    against `scoring_v4/schemas/dose_reference_semantics.schema.json`. There is
    no pending-anchor allow-list. Sean's approved policy and factual dispositions
    are recorded in `docs/plans/DOSE_V41_REFERENCE_DECISIONS.md`.
  - The Dose explanation carries a `dose_receipt`: daily use, contributions
    and aggregation rule, adequacy before safety, the upper reference with its
    population and exposure, the cap and the reconciling final value.
  - Pillar reasons are one sentence; each explanation fact carries
    `audience` (`consumer` | `audit`), with at most one consumer fact per
    pillar (see `scripts/FINAL_EXPORT_SCHEMA_V1.md`).
- Probiotic research certainty counts only when it applies to the product.
- A score is `partial` when the unfinished work is ours (an unresolved
  benchmark identity, or a printed nutrient or sugars amount we could not
  read), never when the label withheld something.

Besides the eight routes, the generic module applies **sub-profiles** for
botanical and collagen products, and generic routing recognizes
**immune-support** and **joint-support** profiles with their own Evidence caps.
Category Evidence caps currently include generic 20, probiotic 20,
multi/prenatal 20, omega 20, immune_support 17, joint_support 14 — always read
the live config rather than trusting this paragraph.

For a scoreable product:

```text
stage3_quality_score_v4_100 = clamp(0, 100, round(sum(pillar.score), 1))
exported_quality_score_v4_100 = floor(stage3_quality_score_v4_100 + 0.5)
```

Category adapters map module evidence to an achievable, purpose-fit pillar
scale. The public score is the literal six-pillar sum: no post-sum cap, clamp
or category adjustment may change it (locked 2026-09-21; the former sports
caps and `quality_score_cap_v4` were removed). A category concern is expressed
by the pillar or safety gate that owns it. Stage 3 retains one decimal for auditability and tier derivation; final export
ships the canonical public score as a half-up whole number and keeps the
decimal analytical value out of the public database.

Clean-label flags remain orthogonal to regulatory verdicts: they may produce a
small bounded Formula & quality checks deduction and consumer explanation without
claiming that a legal additive is a banned ingredient.

### 9.1 Tiers

Tier bands live in `scoring_v4/config/quality_score.json` (`tiers`) and are
currently:

| Shipped whole score | Tier |
|---:|---|
| 95–100 | Exceptional |
| 90–94.9 | Excellent |
| 80–89.9 | Very good |
| 70–79.9 | Good |
| 55–69.9 | Needs improvement |
| 0–54.9 | Poor |

Tier is derived from the shipped whole number (`shipped_whole_score`, half-up
round of the one-decimal total), never from the decimal total, so the hero-card
number and its tier can never disagree. Tier is null when the public score is
suppressed or not scored.

## 10. Independent quality, safety and publication status

`quality_tier` owns the quality rating shown above. `product_safety_status`
independently reports `blocked`, `unsafe`, `caution`,
`no_known_catalog_concern` or `not_assessed`. A quality tier never changes safety;
banned/recalled reasons remain attached to blocked products. The production
owners are `quality_score::assemble_quality_score` and
`scored_artifact::_product_safety_status`, respectively.

Legacy `verdict` remains a compatibility/readiness field. Its declared reader
precedence still includes POOR for older cached catalogs; current scoring never
emits POOR or converts a quality threshold crossing into a safety change.
Completeness policy may impose a tested cap or legacy CAUTION readiness ceiling
without becoming a safety finding. Release safety comparisons and Flutter safety
rendering consume the typed `product_safety_status` field.

| Status | Current compatibility verdicts | Public number |
|---|---|---|
| `scored` | SAFE, CAUTION | finite |
| `suppressed_safety` | BLOCKED, UNSAFE | null |
| `not_scored` | NOT_SCORED | null |

## 11. Dose and UL contract

### 11.1 Exposure range

- Source exposure records retain `per_day_min` for minimum directed daily use and `per_day_max` for maximum directed daily use.
- Every route's Dose runs through `generic_helpers.score_directed_daily_dose`: adequacy uses the minimum directed daily use, safety the maximum, and `metadata.daily_use` records the interval and whether it was defaulted (one serving a day without directions). The safety gate and Dose read one typed `DoseSafetyResult`. Neither establishes a user's actual intake.
- Original source quantities, directions, reference profiles and unknown states remain unchanged. Ambiguous directions or unread amounts never become invented exposures.
- all units enter the shared canonicalizer before conversion
- demographic references are retained in `data_by_group`
- `reference_profile` identifies the adult-neutral compatibility summary

UL selection for catalog screening (Dose V4.1 §2.5, Sean D2) is
`rda_ul_calculator.UL_SCREENING_POPULATIONS`, chosen per product by
`SupplementEnricherV3._ul_screening_population`: with no stated population,
the lowest established UL across ordinary adult groups (calcium 2,000 mg,
phosphorus 3,000 mg); a DSLD life-stage tag narrows it (seniors 51+,
pregnancy/lactation 19-50). A label stating several populations screens the
lowest UL across all of them, and a negated tag ("Women (not pregnant or
lactating)") is an ordinary adult. DSLD's generic 18-50 adult tag does not narrow.
Pediatric UL rows are not in the reference data: the adult limit still
confirms an exceedance and the row records the gap. Adequacy references are
unchanged. Rows and flags carry `ul_population`, and warnings name it.

### 11.2 Folate lineage

This pipeline applies the folic-acid UL only to an identified folic-acid
contribution.

| Label evidence | Adequacy | Folic-acid UL |
|---|---|---|
| Explicit folic acid contribution | Use verified DFE/exposure rule | Compare identified folic-acid contribution |
| Explicit mcg DFE folinic/folinate/leucovorin | Retain DFE adequacy | Not applicable |
| Bare mcg folinic/folinate/leucovorin | Unknown; `scoring_eligible=false` | Not applicable; do not guess a folinic UL |
| Unknown-form folate | Use only supported adequacy evidence | Indeterminate; material cases emit a review flag |

Absent or inconsistent `%DV` is not a conversion. Any future `%DV` recovery
must be a named rule with consistency checks and dedicated tests.

### 11.3 One product-level Dose ceiling

`DoseSafetyResult.dose_ceiling` (config `dose_safety_ceiling`) takes the
strongest confirmed exceedance at maximum directed daily use and
`quality_score._pillar_dose` applies it once, after route aggregation and
normalization:

| Confirmed exposure vs applicable upper reference | Public Dose |
|---|---:|
| ≤100% (exactly 100% is not an exceedance) | no ceiling |
| >100% and <150% | at most 10 |
| ≥150% | 0 |
| Total declared daily caffeine >400 and <600 mg | at most 10 |
| Total declared daily caffeine ≥600 mg | 0 |

The bands and points are PharmaGuide scoring policy, not toxicity grades.
The ceiling never raises a lower score, cannot be diluted by other nutrients
on a panel and counts incidental nutrients. `material_but_unresolved` keeps
its review state with no ceiling. No module subtracts for an exceedance: the
per-nutrient UL bands and the B7 subtraction are retired, and
`B7_safety_evaluation` stays as metadata. The receipt itemizes the ceiling as
a `cap` entry.

## 12. Confidence and provenance

Every v4 result carries:

- scoring engine version
- classification schema version
- module route
- scoring mode
- every consumed rubric version/fingerprint
- confidence band and drivers for eligible products
- gate-specific blocked confidence for hard failures

Provenance is emitted even for early safety/completeness returns so a dispute
can reconstruct the exact policy state.

## 13. Stage-3 artifact boundary

`scoring_v4/scored_artifact.py` is the only production seam that turns one
enriched product into one scored product artifact. It:

1. invokes `score_product_v4()` exactly once
2. obtains coverage and strict diagnostics from `scoring_input_contract.py`
3. verifies completeness coverage agrees with that shared result
4. applies public verdict precedence, including the `<0.3` trust floor
5. emits the v4 score, six pillars, independent product-safety and
   quality-assessment states, provenance, and compatibility mirrors
6. contains hard-block suppression so every public/reserved surface agrees

`score_products_v4.py` owns only batch discovery, validation, atomic writes,
and summary reporting. `build_final_db.py` validates and exports the artifact;
it does not rescore or overlay another result.

The scored artifact projects `delivery_data` from its formulation module’s single eligibility result. Export uses that projection rather than restoring raw/enriched delivery tiers. Chemistry, presentation and an already-assessed preparation cannot earn a second delivery bonus. Definitions and preparation relationships remain in the existing `enhanced_delivery.json`; no Verification or bio-score magnitude changes are implied.

## 14. Export contract

`build_final_db.py` owns final schema validation and quarantine.

- Read the current export schema and supported import versions from
  `build_final_db.py::EXPORT_SCHEMA_VERSION` and `core_export_model.py`.
- Read current `products_core` columns and app/server projections from
  `core_export_model.py::PRODUCTS_CORE_COLUMNS`; do not pin a drifting count here.
- Ranking and dedup use `quality_score_v4_100` only for status `scored`.
- Suppressed safety rows may ship with null score and visible verdict/evidence.
- Verified NOT_SCORED labels may publish with null quality
  score, mirrors, pillars and tier, a truthful assessment reason, and applicable
  warnings; invalid or unverified payloads are quarantined.
- Detail blobs emit `quality_pillars_v4`, score status, provenance, typed
  `v4_dose_safety`, safety-gate state, confidence, and category explanations.
- Deprecated `/80` export columns are forbidden.

## 15. Change control

Every scoring, identity, safety, RDA/UL, or config change requires:

1. a RED regression at the owning boundary
2. the smallest owner-level implementation
3. focused GREEN verification through `scripts/test.sh fast`
4. full fast verification at the cluster boundary
5. release/full profiles before publication
6. a fresh candidate snapshot
7. per-product score/status/verdict/module/pillar review
8. explicit expected-change records
9. reviewed scoring snapshot re-freeze

No aggregate score summary can replace per-product review. No clinical data
identifier is accepted without content verification against its primary source.

## 16. Required regression surfaces

At minimum, preserve coverage for:

- router precedence and cross-category misroutes
- safety signal normalization and jurisdiction applicability
- hard suppression/null-score behavior
- completeness and mapped-coverage ownership
- malformed-row containment
- proprietary blend dedupe and display-only linkage
- unit canonicalization and serving ranges
- folate/folinic adequacy and UL lineage
- v4 pillar math and scored-artifact assembly
- absence of deprecated `/80` fields
- per-product scoring snapshots

Supported test entrypoints:

```bash
scripts/test.sh fast
scripts/test.sh release
scripts/test.sh full
```
